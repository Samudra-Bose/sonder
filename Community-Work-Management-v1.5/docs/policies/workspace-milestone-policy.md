# Workspace Milestone Policy — DEC-OPEN-08

**Status:** OWNER CONFIRMED — 31 October 2026. **API:** 0.3.20-draft.

Milestones remain optional Workspace-level planning markers. They can have a name, description and target date and may be linked from tasks for Timeline/calendar context. They are never hierarchy containers, never an intermediate layer between Workspace and Task, and do not replace task dependencies. They are Workspace-scoped and governed by current Workspace membership/capabilities.

No milestone-specific analytics, cross-Workspace milestone relationships or project/container semantics are implied.
